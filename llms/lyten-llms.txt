# Lyten

> Lyten is a San Jose, California advanced-materials company founded in 2015 that invented Lyten 3D Graphene — a tunable three-dimensional carbon supermaterial produced by converting methane into nanostructured carbon while co-producing hydrogen. Lyten applies that platform to lithium-sulfur batteries (no cobalt, nickel or manganese), 3D printer filament, concrete admixture and structural adhesives, and chemical/environmental sensors including the Lyten Atmos continuous methane-intelligence platform. Lyten publishes no public developer API, SDK, or developer portal; this file describes the company's public web surface only.
